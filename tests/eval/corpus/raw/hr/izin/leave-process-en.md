# Annual Leave Request Process

Employees of Nova Trading UK Ltd submit annual leave requests through the HR portal. This document covers the request, approval, and cancellation flow.

## Submitting a Request

Requests must be submitted **at least 5 business days in advance** of the leave start date through the HR portal. Exceptions are only made for documented medical emergencies; late requests submitted without supporting evidence are automatically rejected by the system.

### Mandatory Form Fields

- Start and end date
- Leave type (annual, compassionate, unpaid)
- Delegated colleague for handover

## Approval Flow

The request first goes to the line manager. If the manager does not respond within 2 business days, the request is automatically escalated to the department head. Unlike some other Nova subsidiaries, HR sign-off is required in addition to manager approval before the request is confirmed and passed to payroll.

## Cancellation and Changes

An approved leave request can be cancelled by the employee through the portal up until the start date. After the start date has passed, cancellation requires manual intervention by HR.

## Carry-Over and Unused Leave

Unused annual leave can be carried over until the end of January of the following year. Any balance not carried over by that date is forfeited and is not paid out in cash.
