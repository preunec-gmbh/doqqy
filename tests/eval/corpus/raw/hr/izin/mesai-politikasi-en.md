# Overtime Policy

This document sets out the complete overtime policy for Nova Trading UK Ltd employees, covering eligibility, approval, compensation, and tracking.

## Eligibility

Overtime policy applies differently depending on employment classification.

### Hourly Staff

Warehouse and logistics staff paid on an hourly basis are eligible for overtime pay for any hours worked beyond their contracted weekly hours, regardless of role or seniority.

### Salaried Staff

Salaried office staff are only eligible for overtime compensation if their role is classified as "non-exempt" in the HR system. Exempt roles, typically management grades, do not receive overtime pay but may be granted time off in lieu at the discretion of their department head.

### Contractors

External contractors are never eligible for overtime under this policy. Any additional hours are governed entirely by the terms of their individual service agreement, not by internal HR policy.

## Approval Process

### Pre-Approval Requirement

All overtime, whether planned or unplanned, must be pre-approved by a line manager before it is worked. Unapproved overtime hours logged in the timesheet system are flagged for review and are not automatically paid.

### Emergency Overtime

In the case of unplanned emergency overtime, such as a warehouse system outage requiring after-hours support, verbal approval from a manager is sufficient at the time, but must be confirmed in writing within 24 hours through the HR portal.

### Weekend and Holiday Overtime

Overtime worked on weekends is compensated at 1.5 times the standard hourly rate. Overtime worked on public holidays is compensated at 2 times the standard hourly rate. These multipliers apply only to hourly staff; non-exempt salaried staff receive time off in lieu at the equivalent rate instead of a pay multiplier.

## Compensation Details

### Payment Timing

Approved overtime pay is included in the payroll run for the month in which it was worked, provided the timesheet was submitted and approved before the monthly payroll cutoff date. Overtime approved after the cutoff is carried over to the following month's payroll.

### Time Off in Lieu

Where time off in lieu (TOIL) is granted instead of pay, it must be used within 8 weeks of being earned. TOIL that is not used within this window is forfeited and cannot be converted to a cash payment.

## Tracking System

All overtime hours are logged through the same timesheet system used for regular hours, with a distinct "Overtime" category. The system automatically calculates the applicable multiplier based on the day of the week and whether it falls on a recognised public holiday, cross-referencing the company holiday calendar maintained by HR.

### Disputes

If an employee disagrees with the multiplier or hours calculated by the system, a dispute can be raised through the HR portal. Disputes are reviewed by the payroll team within 5 business days, and any correction is applied in the next available payroll cycle rather than as an off-cycle payment.

## Review Cycle

This policy is reviewed annually by HR in conjunction with Finance, typically in the first quarter of the calendar year, to account for changes in minimum wage legislation and any renegotiated union agreements covering hourly staff.

## Overtime Rate Reference by Location

Nova Trading UK Ltd operates three sites — the London head office, the Manchester distribution centre, and the Bristol distribution centre — and while the core overtime policy described above applies uniformly in principle, the effective hourly multiplier differs slightly across sites because each site inherited its overtime terms from a different pre-acquisition employer before consolidating under Nova Trading UK Ltd's current payroll system. HR and Finance have agreed not to harmonise these historical differences immediately, since doing so would require renegotiating collective agreements at two of the three sites, and instead maintain a per-site reference table that payroll consults whenever the automatic multiplier calculation in the timesheet system needs a manual override.

At the London head office, which was Nova Trading UK Ltd's original site before the two distribution centres were added through acquisition, the standard weekend multiplier of 1.5x and the public holiday multiplier of 2x described earlier in this policy apply without modification, and no site-specific override exists anywhere in the payroll system for London-based staff, hourly or salaried.

At the Manchester distribution centre, inherited from a 2019 acquisition, warehouse and logistics staff are entitled to an additional 0.25x night-shift premium for any overtime hours worked between 22:00 and 06:00, stacked on top of the standard weekend or holiday multiplier where applicable. This premium does not apply to office-based roles at the same site, only to warehouse and logistics staff paid on an hourly basis, and it is tracked as a separate line item in the timesheet system rather than being folded into the base multiplier, so that payroll disputes about the night-shift premium can be resolved independently of disputes about the underlying weekend or holiday multiplier itself.

At the Bristol distribution centre, inherited from a 2021 acquisition, the collective agreement in place at the time of acquisition specifies a flat 1.75x multiplier for all overtime regardless of whether it falls on a weekday, weekend, or public holiday, which is higher than the standard weekday rate elsewhere but lower than the standard public holiday rate elsewhere. Bristol does not have a separate night-shift premium; the flat 1.75x is considered by the original collective agreement to already account for unsociable hours, and payroll has been advised by Legal not to introduce one without a formal renegotiation.

Cross-site secondments — where a Manchester or Bristol employee is temporarily assigned to cover shortages at another site for a period of days or weeks — use the overtime terms of the site where the hours are physically worked, not the employee's home site. The timesheet system requires the approving manager to select the correct site code manually for any secondment overtime entry, since the automatic multiplier calculation keys off the employee's home site by default and will silently apply the wrong rate if the site code is left unmodified during a secondment.

| Site | Standard Weekday | Weekend | Public Holiday | Night-Shift Premium |
|------|-------------------|---------|-----------------|----------------------|
| London (HQ) | 1.0x | 1.5x | 2.0x | None |
| Manchester | 1.0x | 1.5x | 2.0x | +0.25x (22:00-06:00, warehouse/logistics only) |
| Bristol | 1.75x (flat) | 1.75x (flat) | 1.75x (flat) | None (folded into flat rate) |

Payroll reviews this table annually alongside the main policy review described above, and any change to a site's historical multiplier requires sign-off from both HR and the site's original collective bargaining representative body, not just the standard annual review process that governs the rest of this policy.
