# Invoice Cancellation Process

This document describes how a sales invoice issued by Nova Trading UK Ltd, the UK subsidiary of Nova Ticaret, can be cancelled.

## When Cancellation Applies

Cancellation is used when:

- The customer withdraws the order before dispatch
- A pricing or quantity error is found on the invoice
- A duplicate invoice was raised for the same order

An invoice can only be cancelled while it is still in **draft submission state** with HMRC's Making Tax Digital gateway. Once submitted, a credit note must be issued instead.

## Cancellation Steps

### 1. Raising the Request

The accounts clerk opens the invoice record in the ERP system and selects "Request Cancellation". A cancellation reason is a mandatory field on the form.

### 2. Approval Routing

Cancellation requests are routed to different approvers depending on invoice value:

- Below £2,000: approval from the **Regional Manager** is sufficient.
- £2,000 and above: approval from the **Finance Director** is required.

Requests left unapproved for more than 48 hours are automatically escalated to the Head of Finance.

### 3. Closing the Record

Once approved, the ERP system marks the invoice as "Cancelled" and reverses the associated stock movements. This step cannot be undone.

## Approval Matrix

| Role | Approval Limit | Turnaround |
|------|-----------------|------------|
| Accounts Clerk | No approval authority | - |
| Regional Manager | Up to £2,000 | 48 hours |
| Finance Director | Unlimited | 72 hours |

## Common Pitfalls

If the invoice has already been submitted to HMRC, the cancel button is disabled and the user is redirected to the credit note workflow instead. This behaviour is intentional and exists for regulatory compliance.
